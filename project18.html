Visual identity and logo design for Citywide, a new mobile coffee stand based at the Irving Square Farmers Market.

The design draws inspiration from the owner's creative practice, resulting in an organic, fluid, and distinctive visual identity. The logo blends hand-drawn, expressive elements into a globby, imperfect form that reflects the brand's unique character and personality while emphasizing Citywide's mission to foster human connection through coffee.
