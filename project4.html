CURRENTS Magazine is a publication dedicated to uncovering and reporting on global issues that often go unnoticed or underreported in mainstream media. With a mission to amplify marginalized voices and bring attention to stories that matter, CURRENTS serves as a platform for awareness, dialogue, and change.

The magazine's cover designs are crafted to visually represent the essence of each issue's theme.
