TABU –
Typeface
2024
Type Design
Motion
TABU is a meticulously crafted regular-weight sans serif typeface inspired by my deep appreciation for the aesthetic and storytelling of horror movies, particularly The Blair Witch Project.

The typeface embodies the raw qualities of blending organic textures, such as sticks and branches, with a clean, minimalist sans serif form. The "stick-bound" design evokes imagery of forest rituals—a recurring element in horror films.
