Holiday Party 2025
Holiday Party assets for the Daily Mail's holiday party in 2025. The assets include a digital party invitation, a double-sided printed special drink menu, drink coasters, and matchboxs for attendees to take. The design is modern, minimalist, and visually appealing, with a focus on the brand's identity and the locations/event's theme.

Event assets were developed in close collaboration with the Creative Director.
